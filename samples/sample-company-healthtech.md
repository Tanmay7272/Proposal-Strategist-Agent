# Company Profile: CarePoint Digital Health

CarePoint Digital Health is a 180-person clinical engagement software and integration firm specializing in high-throughput digital intake, patient self-service, and EHR interoperability for large hospital networks.

## Strengths & Differentiators
- **Pre-Built Bi-Directional FHIR Connectors:** Certified App Orchard integration for Epic and validated Cerner SMART-on-FHIR connectors, enabling direct data ingestion without custom intermediary middleware.
- **Zero-Friction "App-Less" Mobile Intake:** Web-based responsive check-in delivered via encrypted SMS and QR codes, achieving an average 74% patient pre-registration adoption rate across current client health systems.
- **Dual-Engine Offline Intake Architecture:** Local tablet/kiosk encrypted queue that caches registrations and automatically syncs when connectivity is restored, proven in rural health settings.
- **HITRUST & SOC 2 Type II:** Full annual HITRUST CSF certification and clean SOC 2 Type II attestation report.

## Past Performance (Citable Evidence)
1. **Tri-State Regional Medical Network (14 Hospitals, 52 Clinics):**
   - Replaced paper intake with CarePoint Digital Intake across 14 facilities within 10 months.
   - Reduced front-desk lobby check-in time from 16 minutes to 2.8 minutes per patient.
   - Achieved 71% mobile pre-registration adoption within 90 days of go-live.
   - Zero unplanned downtime recorded across 14 consecutive site cutovers. Reference contact: Dr. Rebecca Martinez, Chief Medical Information Officer.
2. **Mercy Valley Healthcare (9 Community Hospitals):**
   - Migrated 4.2 million legacy patient registration records into dual Epic/Cerner environment with 99.997% data integrity.
   - Accelerated front-end co-pay collections by 38% via real-time 270/271 eligibility checking. Reference contact: Marcus Vance, VP Revenue Cycle.
3. **Appalachian Rural Health Consortium:**
   - Deployed offline-resilient intake kiosks across 22 broadband-constrained mountain clinics with 99.8% deferred sync reliability.
