# Request for Proposal: Fleet Operations Platform Modernization

**Issuer:** Metro Logistics Authority (MLA)
**RFP No.:** MLA-2026-014
**Response due:** 15 December 2026

## 1. Background

MLA coordinates dispatch, routing, and maintenance for 1,200 municipal delivery and service vehicles across 14 depots. Our current fleet management system was built in 2013 and is run by three separate vendors. Dispatchers re-key work orders between systems, and a failed overnight data sync in March left 9 depots without route assignments for a full morning shift.

The Board has directed us to retire the legacy platform within 18 months without interrupting daily service. Any outage during the transition is a political risk for the Authority.

## 2. Scope of Work

The selected vendor will:

1. Deliver a unified dispatch, routing, and maintenance platform replacing all three legacy systems.
2. Migrate 10 years of vehicle, maintenance, and work-order history.
3. Integrate with MLA's existing fuel-card provider and the city's GIS service.
4. Train 220 dispatchers, mechanics, and depot managers.
5. Provide 24/7 support for 3 years after go-live.

## 3. Mandatory Requirements

- M1. Hosting must be within the country, with data residency documented.
- M2. Platform must support role-based access control and full audit logging.
- M3. Zero unplanned downtime during depot cutovers.
- M4. Vendor must name a dedicated project manager with at least 5 years of public-sector delivery experience.
- M5. Offline-capable mobile app for mechanics working in depots with unreliable connectivity.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Technical approach and migration plan | 35% |
| Relevant past performance and references | 25% |
| Team qualifications | 15% |
| Price | 25% |

## 5. Submission

Proposals must include an executive summary (maximum 1 page), technical approach, management plan, past performance (3 references), and a pricing schedule.

## 6. Notes

MLA staff have said informally that they are tired of vendors who "promise a big bang and deliver a weekend of chaos." Incumbent vendor contracts expire in 24 months.
